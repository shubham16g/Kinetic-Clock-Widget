Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in connection with your use of the Advanced Widget mobile application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal information is kept safe.

1. Information Collection and Use

Our App is built with privacy at its core. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal user data.

A. Device Permissions and Wallpaper Customization

To provide custom visual styles, such as the wallpaper blur effect that places a blurred version of your current wallpaper behind the clock widget, the App requires read-only access to your device's wallpaper:

  • Storage Access: We request read-only access to your device's external storage (via standard system permissions like READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on compatible Android versions) to retrieve the active system wallpaper drawable.
  • Local On-Device Processing: The retrieved wallpaper image is processed (scaled and blurred) and rendered strictly on your local device.
  • No Transmission: The wallpaper image and any associated data never leave your device. We do not upload, transmit, or share your wallpaper image or any other local files to external servers or third parties.

Security Note: All image rendering and customization are performed locally within the Android application sandbox. No external network requests are made with your device files.

B. Precise Timing and Clock Synchronization

  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM): The App uses the exact alarm permission to schedule minute-by-minute system ticks, ensuring the Kinetic clock widget updates its hands and digits precisely in real-time. This permission does not collect or store any personal user information.

2. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise disclose your personal data, local files, or usage information to any third parties. All operations are processed locally on your physical device.

3. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party advertising networks, user analytics tracking SDKs, or crash-reporting services that gather personal or behavioral identifiers.

4. Children's Privacy

Because the App does not collect any personal information whatsoever, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and does not target or knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be posted directly to this document. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about our privacy practices.
